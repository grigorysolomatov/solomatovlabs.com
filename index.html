Spectral imaging

Multispectral and hyperspectral cameras record a spectrum at every pixel instead of three colors, so materials that look alike in RGB can be told apart. Work includes glare removal and translucency maps from multispectral cubes, camera spectral sensitivity, and spectral rendering.

550 nm

The 24 ColorChecker patches from their measured reflectance spectra (400–700 nm, 10 nm bands). Pick a patch to see its spectrum; move the slider to view the chart in a single band.

Codewords are the values of polynomials of degree below k at n points of a finite field. The code is linear, so a message m is encoded as mG; it corrects up to (n−k)/2 errors. Used in QR codes and CDs.
